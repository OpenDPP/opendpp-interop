live, claims-checked site language. Keep the claims discipline intact: trademark **™**

**Boilerplate (the "About" paragraph — press, footers, channel descriptions)**

> OpenDPP™ is a B2B Digital Product Passport platform built for the EU's Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR 2024/1781) — battery passports first, under the 18 February 2027 mandate. It turns product data into standards-conformant, scannable passports: GS1 Digital Link resolution, eIDAS advanced electronic seals, and dual machine-readable output as Asset Administration Shell (AAS v3.0/3.1) and UNTP / W3C Verifiable Credentials. The interoperability schemas, samples, and validators are open, so your passports stay portable and verifiable with off-the-shelf tooling; the security-critical node — key vault, sealing, and credential issuance — stays managed, so signing keys never leave the vault. OpenDPP is a product of Opendpp UAB, Vilnius, Lithuania. "OpenDPP" is a trademark of Opendpp UAB.
